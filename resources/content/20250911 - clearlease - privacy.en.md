## The essentials

We build software for lease contracts, and trust is our most important principle. That is why this website collects as little data as possible.

-   Our own pages set no cookies and store nothing in your browser.
-   We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services and no social media plugins.
-   We serve fonts, images and scripts ourselves. No third party is involved apart from our hosting provider.
-   The only exception is the booking calendar on the "Call" page. It comes from Cal.com and loads only when you open it with a click.
-   We receive personal data when you book a call, write to us, phone us or apply for a job with us.
-   Did you get an email from us and wonder where we found your address? The section "If we have written to you" has the answer.

The following sections explain each processing activity in detail. They apply to the website www.clearlea.se and to our contact with prospects, business partners and applicants, including by email.

## Booking a call through Cal.com

You book a call with us through the service Cal.com. The provider is Cal.com, Inc., 2261 Market Street #4382, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA.

On our "Call" page you can display the Cal.com booking calendar directly. As long as you do not click "Show calendar", no data flows to Cal.com. Only with your click does your browser load the calendar from Cal.com's servers. Cal.com then receives technical data such as your IP address, details of your browser and device, and the page you opened. The embedded calendar sets a cookie from the service provider Cloudflare (`__cf_bm`). Cal.com uses it to fend off automated access. It is not used for advertising or analytics and expires after a short time. In some places we also link directly to a booking page at Cal.com.

When you book a call, Cal.com processes what you enter in the booking form. This includes your name, email address, company, the estimated number of your leases, the time you chose and optional details such as a phone number, further participants and your topic. We use this data to prepare for the call and to hold it with you.

The legal basis for the booking is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to arrange calls with prospects easily. If you yourself are our future contract partner, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. We base loading the calendar, and the cookie it needs, on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). You expressly request the calendar with your click, and we could not display it without this technology.

Cal.com also processes data in the USA. Cal.com, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The transfer therefore rests on Art. 45 GDPR.

You can find more in Cal.com's privacy policy at [https://cal.com/privacy](https://cal.com/privacy).
