FAQ

What teams like yours ask first.

Does clearlea.se replace our ERP?
No. clearlea.se delivers verified data to your ERP, your CREM and your data warehouse, via API and standard formats. With clean data, these systems finally reach their full potential.
How fast is a portfolio captured?
A lease management service provider took on a portfolio of more than 600 sites, with an average of 10 documents per site. Processing took under 2 hours. Including quality checks by people, it took 3 days instead of 10 weeks.
Can we reuse data from the acquisition?
Yes. What you capture in due diligence becomes your portfolio data after closing, with history and source. And you can export all data at any time and hand it over to your systems. No vendor lock-in.
How confidential is transaction data?
A dedicated instance per customer, strict client separation, and on request private cloud, on-premises or fully isolated. More in the Trust Center
Do you also take on one-off transaction projects?
Yes. For valuation specialists and transaction managers, we also work on a project basis. Ask Hieronymus Deutsch for a tailored quote.
