FAQ

What teams like yours ask first.

Do we have to switch our property management software?
No. clearlea.se works alongside your software, for example iX-Haus or Yardi, and hands over data via API or a standard format. With clean data, you get more out of your software. No vendor lock-in: you can export your data at any time.
How do we get the previous manager’s data?
As a data room, an export or via a mailbox. clearlea.se classifies everything and shows what is missing.
Can owners and service providers collaborate?
Yes. External parties take on individual steps, with a deadline and a log. With guest access, they work in your shared workspace, including comments and dashboards.
