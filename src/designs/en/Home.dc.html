Sovereignty & security

Your data, your rules. In your own infrastructure if you wish.

For procurement, IT security and data protection: the key answers before you ask.

  1. Managed CloudA dedicated instance per customer, hosted in Germany in ISO 27001-certified data centres. If you wish, with no US providers at all, AI included.
  2. Private CloudIn your cloud environment, run by your IT. Documented and enterprise-ready.
  3. On-premisesOn your own servers, with your security policies.
  4. Air-gappedCan run fully isolated, with no outside connection.
Does our data go to the US or China?
No. Data and AI processing stay in Germany or in your own environment. If you wish, with no US providers at all.
Do you train your AI on our data?
No. Neither we nor any model provider uses your data for training.
How good is the data?
98% accuracy, a QA index for every new value and a source down to the clause. We flag uncertain values instead of overwriting them.
What about ISO 27001 and GDPR?
Operated in ISO 27001-certified data centres, our own certification in preparation. Data processing agreement (DPA) under Art. 28 GDPR, TOMs on request.
