The problem

Everyone is building something with AI. What's missing is central control.

Prompts are not processesA prompt gives one answer today and another tomorrow. Internal audit and compliance cannot verify that.
Every team builds on its ownTheir own uploads, their own prompts, their own folders. No central permissions, no shared log.
Long chains lose qualityAt 95% accuracy per step, about 60% is left after ten steps. Agents solve 58% of single tasks, but only 35% of multi-step workflows.Sources: Lusser's law; Salesforce AI Research, CRMArena-Pro (2025)
Agents with a master keyMany agents run with their user's permissions, so they see everything that user sees. Afterwards, it is hard to trace who was allowed to see what.

Agents under supervision

Agents, yes. But within fixed rules.

In clearlea.se, AI steps work inside workflows. Every step has a named owner, and thresholds decide when a human approves. The rules live in code, not in the prompt.

What the AI may do
  • Read and classify documents, propose values
  • Flag deviations and explain them
  • Prepare drafts, such as letters or objections
  • Adopt values above your QA threshold
What the AI may never do
  • Act without permission
  • Override rules or thresholds
  • Change closed cases
  • Run an action twice
Your own agentsComing soonToday your agents connect via MCP and work with the current valid state. Soon agents will also own steps in workflows, with the same guarantees as any other owner.
The 9 guarantees in detail
Governance

One place for permissions, logs and approvals.

Instead of a sprawl of one-off tools: data sources, permissions and workflows managed centrally. That makes AI usable even where auditors look closely.

Separate permissions for every agentEvery workflow step and every agent gets its own permissions. No agent runs with a user's permissions.
Audit trail for everythingEvery action by users, AI and systems: who, when, why.
Versioned workflowsNew versions go live only after review, testing and approval.
QA index, not gut feelingEvery new value comes with a quality signal. Uncertain values get flagged, not overwritten.
