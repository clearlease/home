As of today, clearlea.se is an RICS Tech Network Solution Provider. With the new RICS Tech Network, the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors brings together technology providers that build solutions for the work of surveyors, valuers and property firms. You will find us under Commercial Property and FM.

## Why we take part

We give people back time for work that needs judgement. That takes AI that professionals do not have to trust blindly.

- **Responsibility stays with people.** Since 9 March 2026, the RICS professional standard "Responsible use of artificial intelligence in surveying practice" has been in effect. Whoever relies on an AI output is accountable for it. That is how clearlea.se works: the AI captures, checks and flags. A named person decides and approves.
- **Verifiable answers for valuers and asset managers.** Anyone who values, manages or advises needs the current valid state of a lease, not the first hit in the data room. For every data point, clearlea.se delivers the current valid state across lease, amendments and terminations, with history and a source down to the clause.
- **Learning from each other.** In the network we meet providers driven by the same question: how does the industry use AI responsibly? That is the exchange we are looking for.

## What the network is, and what it is not

clearlea.se is an RICS Tech Network Solution Provider. The RICS Tech Network brings together organisations and thought leaders committed to using technology to address challenges across the built and natural environment and drive innovation across the sector. All services we provide are independent of RICS. Participation in the RICS Tech Network does not constitute or imply any certification, accreditation, approval, recommendation, endorsement or verification by RICS of our products or services.
