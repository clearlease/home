Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude are fast. But they only know what you give them. If an amendment is missing from the upload, they answer anyway.

With the clearlea.se MCP server, your assistant queries the current valid state directly. clearlea.se answers with the verified value, its history and the source. No uploads, no context window full of PDFs, no guessing about missing amendments.

## What changes for your team

- **One query, not whole documents.** One call with about 2,000 tokens instead of 120,000 for five leases (example calculation).
- **Same question, same answer.** The answer comes from verified data, not from a prompt.
- **Access controlled centrally.** You decide in one place which AI may see what, just as for people and automations.
- **Answers you can verify.** Every value with its source, down to the clause. Your AI cites instead of guessing.

## Works with your assistants

Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, automations such as n8n and any client that supports MCP. The MCP server is part of the platform.
