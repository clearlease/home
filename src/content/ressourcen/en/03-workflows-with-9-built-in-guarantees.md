A prompt gives one answer today and another tomorrow. Internal audit and compliance cannot verify that.

Workflows in clearlea.se break a business process into steps. AI prepares or completes the work, a named person is accountable, with a deadline and escalation.

## The rules live in the code, not in the prompt

A model can ignore anything that only lives in the prompt. That is why every workflow comes with nine guarantees:

1. No change without approval
2. No action runs twice
3. No decision on outdated data
4. Closed cases stay unchanged
5. Complete audit trail
6. Strict client separation, least privilege
7. GDPR deletion without losing the audit trail
8. No faulty workflow goes live
9. AI never overrides the rules

## Proactive in the background

Workflows start on their own, around the clock. Nobody has to write a prompt first. Specialist approval is a step of its own, with signature and justification, logged audit-proof.
